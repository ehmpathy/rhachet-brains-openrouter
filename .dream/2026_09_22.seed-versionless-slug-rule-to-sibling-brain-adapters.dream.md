# 🌙 dream: seed the versionless-slug rule to the 7 sibling brain adapters

kind: reseed
owner: unclaimed
caught: 2026-09-22

## .the cue that fired

fireworks retired 5 of this package's 12 models at once. two could be routed, three could
not. the fix that makes it cost a consumer naught is a **versionless slug**
(`fireworks/{family}/{tier}/latest`), and the practice was enruled as
`rule.require.versionless-slug-per-tier`.

the wisher's instruction was general — *"enrule to always make a generic versionless option
to prevent churn going forward"*. the rule as written is scoped to **this repo only**, at
`.agent/repo=.this/role=any/briefs/`. the other 7 adapters cannot see it.

## .the gap, precisely

8 brain adapters exist under `ehmpathy/rhachet-brains-*`:

```
rhachet-brains-anthropic    rhachet-brains-bhrain      rhachet-brains-chutes
rhachet-brains-fireworksai  rhachet-brains-openai      rhachet-brains-togetherai
rhachet-brains-typesafeai   rhachet-brains-xai
```

each exports version-pinned slugs as its public contract, so each carries the identical
hazard: when its provider retires a version, every downstream consumer breaks.

**checkable evidence, measured 2026-09-22:**

- `rhx git.repo.get repos --repos 'ehmpathy/rhachet-brains-*'` → 8 repos
- `rhachet-brains-bhrain` calls `genAtomFireworks({ slug: 'fireworks/deepseek/v4-flash' })`
  at `src/domain.operations/arch1/core/invokeBrainArch1.fireworksai.deepseek-v4-flash.codewrite.integration.test.ts:192`,
  and its `readme.md:203` recommends that slug — a real consumer that this repo's route just
  saved. the next such consumer may name a package that has no route.
- `rhachet-brains-xai`'s `define.brain-config-pattern` brief declares slugs like
  `xai/grok/4.1-fast-with-reason` — version in the public slug, no generic peer.

## .the shape of the fix

1. move `rule.require.versionless-slug-per-tier.md` to a **shared** role brief that every
   adapter boots — the same place `define.brain-config-pattern` would live if it were shared,
   rather than duplicated per repo as it is today
2. generalize its text off `fireworks/` onto `{repo}/{family}/{tier}/latest`, so it reads for
   xai, anthropic, openai alike
3. per adapter: add the generic slugs, the `PINNED_BY_LATEST_SLUG` registry, and the
   `asPinnedAtomSlug` resolver. the fireworks implementation is the reference — 4 files:
   `BrainAtom.config.ts` (unions + registry), `BrainAtom.retired.ts`, `asPinnedAtomSlug.ts`,
   `getOneRetirementError.ts`
4. carry the two clamps that earned their keep here, both verified to go red:
   - no generic resolves onto a retired model (a scheduled break under a safe name)
   - no duplicate slug in the published catalog list

🟡 the tier read is per-provider. fireworks encodes its tier in each model's `description`
(`frontier` → pro, `cheapfast`/`cheapest` → flash). another adapter may carry no such word,
in which case the tier must be added to its config before the generics can be assigned —
never guessed.

## .why it is not done in this round

DIRTY, not merely large: the rule's home is a cross-repo placement decision, and steps 3-4
touch 7 separate repos and their published contracts. that ripples well past the diff this
round opened, so it fails the CLEAN test.

⇒ the wisher chose "keep it here, seed later" when asked directly on 2026-09-22.
