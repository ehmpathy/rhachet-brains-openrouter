# case=12 — no credential

**feel** sharp · **care** alterpath (every new consumer meets it once; cheap to recover)

## narrative

jo wires a first ask and forgets the context. jo runs it and gets an instant error, no spend:
`OPENROUTER_API_KEY required`. the error shows jo both ways to supply it — the keyrack
shorthand and the explicit getter — and points to the readme section on key setup. jo pastes
the keyrack line, reruns, and the ask answers.
