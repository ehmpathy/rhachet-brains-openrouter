# case=4 — the promises leave zero endpoints

**feel** sharp · **care** critipath (the forbidden cell lives here: a silent route to an endpoint
that breaks a promise)

## narrative

priya asks on `openrouter/deepseek/flash/region=usa&precision=fp8`. one endpoint serves from the
usa (`fireworks/us`), and it does not declare fp8. so no endpoint keeps both promises. the atom
sends no ask. it throws, and the message shows which promise emptied the set: 33 endpoints,
1 after `region=usa`, 0 after `precision=fp8` — with the one usa endpoint and its quantization
beside it. priya drops `precision=fp8` and the next ask serves.
