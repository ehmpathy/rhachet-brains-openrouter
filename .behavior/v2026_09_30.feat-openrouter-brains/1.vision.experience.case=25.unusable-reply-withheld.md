# case=25 — an unusable 200 reply is withheld, and the floor steps past it

**feel** sharp · **care** critipath (frequent on the cheapest hosts — measured 2026-10-02 on five
of nine large review asks — and silent if read as text: a reviewer lane crashed on a bare
`SyntaxError`, with no host, no generation id, and no cause)

## narrative

the reviewer fleet asks `openrouter/deepseek/flash/floor&speed=min50tps&privacy=full` for a long
review. openrouter answers 200, yet the reply is unfit to read. the host failed mid-reply
(`finish_reason=error`), or the body holds an `error` where `choices` belongs, or the reply is
empty where a json schema was owed, or it is prose where json was owed, or it was cut at the
output limit.

on `floor`, the walk treats a failed host as one that did not serve: it steps to the next
cheapest qualified endpoint, never further, and the trail names the hop. where no hop is left,
or the ask took no walk, the atom fails loud with a `MalfunctionError`. the error names the
finish reason, the host, the generation id, and the fix. the caller never reads the broken text.

this is distinct from case=13 (decided before the call, from the endpoints list), case=14 (429 on
every endpoint), and case=22 (a good reply from the wrong host). here the right host answered 200,
and the reply itself is unusable.
