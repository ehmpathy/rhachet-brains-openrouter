# case=21 — `floor` walks past a throttle, in price order

**feel** happy · **care** critipath (a fleet on `floor` is the main use; the cheapest hosts
throttle most, and a fallback to openrouter's balancer cost 1.4–4.9× on 2026-10-01)

## narrative

omar's reviewer fleet fires twelve asks on `openrouter/deepseek/flash/floor&speed=min50tps`. the
cheapest qualified endpoint throttles three of them. each of those three steps, at the cost of one
more round trip, to the **next cheapest** endpoint and serves there. none lands on a host pricier
than it had to. omar's metrics show, per ask, the attempt list in price order. the endpoints read
from the first ask sits on disk, so every later ask in the next half hour reads none.

## [tn] sketch

```
given('[case21] a floor slug, a fleet, and a throttled head endpoint')
  when('[t0] omar's fleet fires twelve asks at once')
    then('in one process, one endpoints read serves all twelve (an in-memory promise per key, F19)')
  when('[t0b] omar's next ci job, a fresh process, asks within 30 minutes')
    then('it reads the endpoints from disk; no network read')
    then('each ask pins the cheapest qualified endpoint first')
  when('[t1] some of omar's asks are throttled (429) on the head endpoint')
    then('each of those asks pins the next endpoint in price order, no fallback')
    then('metrics.route.attempts = a prefix of the cheapest-first order, each with its status')
    then('no attempt lands on an endpoint pricier than the first non-throttled one')
  when('[t2] omar asks while the cached set names an endpoint openrouter now refuses as absent')
    then('the cache entry for the model is dropped and re-read once')
```
