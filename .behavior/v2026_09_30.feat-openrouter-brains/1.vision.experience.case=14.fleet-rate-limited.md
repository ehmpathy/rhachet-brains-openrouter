# case=14 — a fleet hits openrouter's rate limit

**feel** sharp · **care** critipath (a fleet is the main reason to want a cheap route; a throttle
mid-run is frequent, and a hidden one stalls every reviewer at once)

## narrative

omar's ci starts twelve reviewers at once, all on
`openrouter/deepseek/flash/floor&precision=fp8`, all on one key. most answer on the cheapest fp8
endpoint; the ones it throttles walk to the next cheapest and answer there (case=21). four find
every qualified endpoint throttled. omar's log shows four clear lines — rate limited by
openrouter, each endpoint tried and its status, retry after N seconds — not twelve stack traces,
and not four reviewers hung on a silent backoff. his ci retry policy re-runs those four after the
pause and they pass.

two kinds of "try again" live here, and they stay apart: the **walk** to another qualified
endpoint is a route decision the atom owns, since every promise still holds; the **retry** of the
same ask after a pause is a policy the caller owns.

## [tn] sketch

```
given('[case14] twelve concurrent asks on one key, and openrouter throttles')
  when('[t0] omar's fleet fires twelve asks, and every qualified endpoint throttles some of them')
    then('each such ask fails with MalfunctionError: rate limited by openrouter')
    then('the error lists every endpoint tried, in order, with its status')
    then('the error carries retry-after when openrouter sends it')
    then('the other asks succeed, unaffected')
  when('[t1] omar retries the throttled asks after the pause')
    then('they succeed')
  when('[t2] any of omar's asks is throttled on every qualified endpoint')
    then('the atom does not pause and retry on its own; the caller owns that policy')
```

## proof & clamp

| promise | proven by | clamp — goes red when |
