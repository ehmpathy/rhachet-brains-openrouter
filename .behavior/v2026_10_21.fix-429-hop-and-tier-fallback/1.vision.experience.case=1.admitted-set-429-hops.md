# case=1 — admitted set, a 429 hops

feel: happy · care: critipath · actor: caller (rhachet-roles-bhrain cache suite)

## narrative
the cache suite asks `openrouter/deepseek/flash/speed=min100tps&privacy=full` — no floor, so it
admits every qualified host at once and lets openrouter balance. three qualify: A, B, C.
openrouter routes to A. A answers `429 Provider returned error`, `metadata.provider_name: A`,
`retry-after: 7`. today the ask dies. after: A leaves `only`, the ask re-sends with `only: [B, C]`,
B serves. the suite never sees the throttle; `supply.attempts` shows it.
