# case=9 — a cut or stalled host is stepped past, never fatal, never a hang

sharp × critipath. cells: `d7=body-cut × d3=floor` [t1], `d7=stalled × d3=floor` [t2].

## .narrative

measured on this route, 2026-10-21. bhrain ran two peer reviews of the vision on the flash floor
slug, three rounds over. in four of six, the first admitted host failed it: once its body ended
mid-json, so the ask died on a raw `SyntaxError`; three times it never finished, so each review
sat 21 minutes and returned no verdict. openrouter served the whole time; other hosts were one
hop away.

after: the walk treats both as what they are, a host that did not serve. a cut body steps to the
next qualified host at once. a stalled host is aborted at the per-hop bound, body included, then
the walk steps on. the reply arrives from the second host, and the supply report names the first
host's fault, so no one mistakes the wait or the charge for the served host's.

## .fails safe

- **fast** — a cut body steps on with no wait; a stall costs one bound, never the caller's whole
  clock
- **loud** — each fault is an attempt in the supply report (`body-cut`, `stalled`), and an ask
  whose hops all faulted raises the extant floor-exhausted error, which lists each. a walk stops
  after two stalls and names the hosts left untried (F16)
- **teaches** — the exhausted error names the bound, so a caller whose answers are slow, not
  dead, knows the lever is a thought cap, not a longer bound

## .timeline

```
given('[case9] a floor slug with hosts A then B qualified, A first')
  when('[t1] A returns a body that ends mid-json')
    then('the ask does not reject with a SyntaxError')
    then('the walk steps to B, and B serves')
    then('supply.choice.attempts holds A as body-cut, then B as served')
  when('[t2] A sends no whole body inside the per-hop bound')
    then('the hop to A aborts at the bound, body included, and its socket is freed')
    then('the walk steps to B, and B serves')
    then('supply.choice.attempts holds A as stalled')
  when('[t3] every qualified host stalls')
    then('the walk stops after two stalls, and names the hosts left untried (F16)')
    then('the ask rejects with the floor-exhausted error, each host tried listed as stalled')
    then('the fix names the bound, and a thought cap as the lever for a slow answer')
  when('[t4] A answers a 502 (issue #12)')
    then('the walk steps to B, and B serves')
    then('supply.choice.attempts holds A as failed; no cache entry is dropped')
  when('[t5] A ends a json ask with stop and 0 chars (issue #24)')
    then('the walk steps to B, and B serves')
    then('supply.choice.attempts holds A as empty')
    then('A is skipped by json asks on this machine for 7 days, as a prose host is')
```

## .the bound

a reply cut at the output limit (`finish_reason=length`) never steps, empty or not. that is the
all-thought case (case=2): the next host would spend the same thought again, at the same price.
it raises its named error at once. issue #13 asks the walk to step past a cut-off reply too; this
tree keeps that one clause out, on purpose (F12).
