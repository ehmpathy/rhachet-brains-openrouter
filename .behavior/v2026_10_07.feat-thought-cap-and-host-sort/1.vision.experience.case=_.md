# experience inventory — thought cap + host sort

axes: `1.vision.experience.dimensions.md`. a row may hold several cells where every cell in it
shares one verdict and one reason; the cell count is stated.

care = freq × cost. feel = happy | sharp. **forbidden** = an attempt the behavior refuses (a
rejection invariant). no cell here is impossible by nature: every barred cell is an attempt a
caller can make.
