# case=4 — a caller asks for the fastest host first

happy × critipath. cells: `d2=throughput × d3=floor × d5a=absent × d5b=absent` [t1],
`d2=throughput × d3=admitted-set × d5a=absent × d5b=absent` [t2].

## .narrative

bhrain's review lanes race a deadline; a host 3× faster at 2× the price is the better buy. it
chooses the listed slug `openrouter/deepseek/flash/floor&speed=min50tps&privacy=full&sort=throughput`.
the walk still admits one host per hop and still keeps every promise — speed, privacy — but tries
the fastest qualified host first rather than the cheapest. the supply report says the walk ran by
throughput, so no reader mistakes the charge for a floor price.

a caller who writes the sort word alone, `openrouter/deepseek/flash/sort=throughput`, gets the
same walk: a sort word orders hosts and never drops a promise, so it merges onto the default,
zero data retention included (F28).

on a slug whose filters name no `floor` word, openrouter balances within the admitted set; the package forwards
`provider.sort: 'throughput'`, and openrouter tries the admitted hosts fastest-first, and falls to
the next one inside `only` when the top host is throttled.
